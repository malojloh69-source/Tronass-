# Баннер «Tronaccs Трекер»

Использована встроенная генерация изображений (built-in image_gen).
Файл в проекте: `banner.jpg`. Генерация в PNG, затем обычное JPEG-сжатие
для отправки через Telegram. Изображение используется на всех экранах бота.

## Prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: finished wide Telegram bot menu banner,
approximately 16:9 landscape. Primary request: an elegant minimalist banner for
a Telegram listings tracker named Tronaccs Трекер, matching the visual identity
of tronaccs.market: charcoal-black #171718 background, dark graphite #272729
surfaces, amber-gold #F5AF00 accents, warm white typography.

Text (verbatim, exactly two lines): "Tronaccs" and "Трекер". The first word is in
large confident contemporary sans-serif warm-white type, the second is in amber
gold, similarly readable. Exact Cyrillic: Т р е к е р. Use generous clean spacing,
strong hierarchy and excellent small-screen legibility. One refined amber radar /
tracking signal motif made of a few thin concentric rings and a small luminous
dot on the right, with restrained soft light. Flat editorial graphic design with
subtle depth, professionally composed and restrained. No extra text, no slogans,
no numbers, no watermark, no characters, no money, no gift boxes, no busy scattered
objects, no excessive neon, no glossy plastic 3D, no purple, no blue. Keep all text
safely within generous margins; the image must work as the same reusable header
in every Telegram bot menu section.
